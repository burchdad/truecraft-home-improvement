Your project, step by step

Start with a conversation.

You do not need a finished plan. Here is what to have in mind when you talk with Bo about your home.

Call TrueCraft

Reach Bo at (903) 275-0361. Start with the room or outdoor space you want to improve and the changes you have in mind.

Share your ideas

Describe what you would like to change, what you want to keep and any concerns about the existing space. Approximate dimensions can help the conversation.

Discuss your location

Share your town or ZIP and preferred timing. TrueCraft can discuss whether your project and location are a fit and what availability looks like.

Plan the next step

Talk with Bo about the information needed for an estimate. Pricing, scope and scheduling are confirmed separately; a conversation does not book work.

A few helpful details

  • The space you want to improve
  • Your town or ZIP
  • Your priorities for the project
  • Your preferred timing
