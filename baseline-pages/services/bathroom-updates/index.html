Bathroom updates

A bathroom update starts with understanding the room and what you want to improve. Talk with TrueCraft about your bathroom project in Tyler, Flint, Lindale, Chandler, Bullard, Troup, Kilgore, Jacksonville, Mineola, and Athens, Texas.

Plan around the room you have

Describe the existing bathroom, the changes you are considering and any areas that need attention. Distinguish the features you want to retain from those you hope to change. The conversation helps establish which work is a fit before a price or schedule is agreed.

What to share with Bo

  • Which bathroom you want to update and its approximate size.
  • The changes that matter most to you.
  • Any existing damage or concerns you have noticed.
  • Whether another bathroom will be available during the project.
