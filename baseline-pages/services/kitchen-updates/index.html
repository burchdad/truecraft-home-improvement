Start with what you want to change about the kitchen you use every day. TrueCraft works with homeowners on kitchen updates in Tyler, Flint, Lindale, Chandler, Bullard, Troup, Kilgore, Jacksonville, Mineola, and Athens, Texas.

A clearer starting point for your kitchen

You may be looking to update the room’s appearance or improve how the space works for your household. Tell Bo which parts of the kitchen you want to keep and which need attention. Discuss the proposed scope before choosing materials or making plans around a start date.

What to share with Bo

  • The parts of the kitchen you would like to update.
  • What currently feels inconvenient or unfinished.
  • Approximate room dimensions and any ideas you have collected.
  • Whether you will need to use the kitchen during the work.
